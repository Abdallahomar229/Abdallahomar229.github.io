Imposter Night

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short

Imposter Night is an offline party games app. There are no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not collect or store your personal data on any server.

Data that stays on your device

  • Player names you type in.
  • Your settings (sound, narrator, vibration) and game choices.

This information is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us. You can delete it anytime from Settings → Erase data, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads

The free version shows a few full-screen ads between rounds, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address and technical information about your device, as described in Google's privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Imposter Night Plus removes all ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not use the microphone or camera.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your data.

Narrator voice

The Mafia narrator uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Nothing is recorded.

In-app purchases

Imposter Night Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

The content is family-friendly, but the app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
